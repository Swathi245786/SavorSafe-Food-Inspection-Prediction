# Savor Safe: Chicago Food Inspection Analysis

## Project Overview

Savor Safe is a data science project that analyzes Chicago food inspection records to identify food safety patterns, understand inspection outcomes, and explore factors associated with inspection failures.

The project combines exploratory data analysis (EDA), data cleaning, statistical analysis, data visualization, and predictive modeling to generate insights that may support data-driven food safety inspections.

## Objectives

- Explore trends in Chicago food inspection outcomes.
- Analyze inspection failures across facility types, risk levels, and geographic areas.
- Clean and preprocess inspection data for analysis.
- Investigate relationships between inspection characteristics and failure rates.
- Compare predictive models for identifying establishments at higher risk of inspection failure.
- Explore risk-based inspection prioritization.

## Dataset

The project uses Chicago food inspection records containing information such as:

- Inspection dates and results
- Facility types
- Risk classifications
- Inspection types
- Geographic locations
- Recorded food safety violations

## Methodology

### 1. Data Cleaning and Preprocessing
- Handle missing values and inconsistent records.
- Standardize inspection results and categorical variables.
- Prepare features for statistical analysis and predictive modeling.

### 2. Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)
- Examine inspection outcome distributions.
- Analyze trends across time, facility types, and risk levels.
- Explore geographic differences in inspection failure rates.

### 3. Statistical Analysis and Visualization
- Investigate patterns and associations in inspection outcomes.
- Visualize trends, distributions, and potential risk factors.

### 4. Predictive Modeling
- Explore classification approaches for predicting inspection failures.
- Compare model performance using relevant evaluation metrics.
- Analyze predicted risks to support inspection prioritization.
